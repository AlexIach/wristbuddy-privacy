# WristBuddy Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-18
**Responsible for the data:** Alexandru Iachimov, wristbuddyinfo@gmail.com

## In short

WristBuddy is a pet for your watch that grows from your activity. Everything runs on the watch: the app has no server, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not receive your data.

## What data the app uses

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Steps, workouts and sleep from Health (read-only, only if you allow it) | So your pet gets food and experience | On the watch. Never sent anywhere and never written back to Health |
| Pet state: name, meters, experience, food stock | So your pet is kept between launches | On the watch, in the app's file |
| Recent activity totals: daily step totals for the last 14 days (used to work out your personal goal) and workout records (ID, start and end time) for the last 7 days | So the same steps or workout are not counted twice | On the watch, in the app's file |
| Settings: the pet's sleep schedule, step goal, pause | So the app works the way you set it up | On the watch |

We do not request heart rate, location, contacts, photos or any other data.

## What we do not do

- We do not send data off the watch and do not keep it on servers.
- We do not share data with third parties, sell it or use it for advertising.
- We do not track you across other apps and websites.
- We do not collect analytics or crash reports.
- We do not store health data in iCloud.

## Access to Health

The app works without access too: your pet then gets a small food supply by the clock. You can grant or withdraw access at any time: Settings on the watch, then Health, then Apps.

## Storage and deletion

Data stays on the watch until you delete the app or choose "Start over" in Settings. Deleting the app removes the pet's file. A backup of your watch may contain it along with other app data: the system does that, not us.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and teenagers and is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
